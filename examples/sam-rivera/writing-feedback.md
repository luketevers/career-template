## Voice rules (distilled)

- Opens with the point, no wind-up: "The short version is..."
- Short declarative sentences; rarely more than two clauses.
- Uses concrete numbers early; rounds honestly ("about half", "~40 min").
- Parentheses for asides, never footnote-y qualifiers.
- No em dashes, no exclamation points, no "excited to".
- Says "I think" when it's a judgment; states facts without hedging.
